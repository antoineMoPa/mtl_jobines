# Champlain bridge game integration

Source: `../model-harness/models/model-the-montreal-champlain-bridge-model-for-a-game/assembly.glb`.
The source was generated by Claydash. The copied GLB retains every original mesh and material;
a parent node scales the glTF across-road Z axis by 1.4 to fit the game's 4.5 m vehicle lanes.
Length and height remain 520 m and 111 m, respectively. The game importer maps glTF Y up to Z up.

`champlain_model` in `map.road` places the bridge length along negative gameplay Y, from
(12, -30.9) to (12, -36.1), with a 56 m wide footprint. Placement elevation -0.02 aligns the
model's original water datum to the river. Asphalt is at elevation 0.24, and the two authored
carriageways are at 0.2402 to avoid coincident surfaces. Each carriageway has three 4.5 m lanes;
their centres are at X=11.7984 and X=12.2016, clear of both tower shafts and cable anchors.

The placement disables the default solid building-box collision. Authored pavement supplies
vehicle support and road safety barriers. Hermes removes generated slabs and piers beneath
this footprint from the render mesh so the imported structure remains visible. The mainland
approach stays at the cloverleaf overpass height before climbing to the deck; the South Shore
approach descends to ground.
Both approaches taper to the divided highway lane positions and grades.
