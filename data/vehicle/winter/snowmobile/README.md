# Motoneige

Source: `../model-harness/models/a-snowmobile/assembly.claydef`, generated by Claydash.
Exported as direct geometry at resolution 8 without baked AO, joined in Blender,
scaled from millimetres to metres, welded at 0.5 mm, then decimated at ratio 0.15.
The resulting GLB is about 1.6 MiB, with 24 materials and no texture dependency.

Game integration adds two front lenses and one rear lens using existing catalog roles.

Bounds including the rear lens: X -1.38–1.52002 m (nose +X), Y 0–1.22003 m, Z ±0.54994 m.
The importer subtracts the 0.22 m ride height; track and ski bottoms touch ground.
Four support samples approximate the rear track and front skis. The joined mesh
is rigid; track motion and ski steering are not separately animated.

The optional physics traction profile scales acceleration and powered top speed.
Winter grass/sand uses the snow response; summer grass/sand crawls at under 1 m/s.
Pavement uses the road response. Other vehicles retain their existing tire response.
The model is excluded from NPC road traffic and is parked at the chalet. It is also
available through the vehicle selector. Use the season command to select winter.
